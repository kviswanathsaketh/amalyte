Five "crazy" directions for the Amalyte homepage

Click any card to select it — you can pick more than one to blend

1. The Assembly

Scroll-pinned text builds "An idea. Becomes an execution." on black, then the glass sphere appears, real-time draggable + cursor-lit, shatters into shards on completion, then recedes into a 4-orb division constellation you fly through.

2. The Ledger

Real proof — partner names, project counts — fly in as fragments and physically coalesce into the glass mark as you scroll. The brand's own voice rule ("every claim carries a number or a name") becomes the hero mechanic itself.

3. Mirror Divisions

The page is strict black-and-white until your cursor moves — a "lens" around the pointer reveals the division colors hiding underneath. Turns the new black/white-base rule into something the visitor discovers, not just sees.

4. Living Type

Skip the 3D object for the opening beat entirely — the headline itself is rendered in the v8 glass material, refracting light as the cursor passes near each letter. Cheaper to build than a full scene, and nobody else treats headline text as the signature render.

Ship it.

5. Sound of Execution

Opt-in, muted by default: one soft, tactile sound cue exactly when the shatter-and-reform completes. Almost no B2B site uses sound at all — smallest engineering lift here, most likely to actually get remembered.
